# Yiqi Wang | EARNEST Design Application

Portfolio for the Research Laboratory for Advanced Social Robotics. My interests are **robotics and fabrication (Area 1)** and **AI agents in everyday community life (Area 3)**.

- [WindSinger case study and hardware photos](WindSinger.md)
- [CV (PDF)](Wang_Yiqi_EARNEST_CV.pdf)

WindSinger is an ongoing PolyU team prototype that connects a breath-sensing device to game feedback and an app for reviewing practice sessions. I lead hardware research and design as one of four co-PMs and work with the other leads to decide what the team can prototype and test.
